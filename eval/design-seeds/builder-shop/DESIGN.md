# Design direction — Cascade Shop

Mood: bright, energetic, conversion-first retail. Mint paper, deep-green ink, one loud orange.

- Color: the orange (`primary`) is ONLY for buying actions — Add to cart, Checkout, the hero CTA. Everything else stays green or neutral; never two orange buttons side by side.
- Surfaces: white cards (`bg-card`) on the mint page; product photos large and square on a neutral well (`bg-muted`).
- Type: headlines in the display face (Rubik, `font-serif`), bold and large (32 px+); body in Nunito Sans. Short copy: one message per card.
- Layout: generous blocks with 48 px+ gaps; the catalog is a clean grid of 3–4 cards; the price bold and close to the title.
- Page order: hero (value prop + a real product photo) → product grid → three benefits → closing CTA band. Repeat the buy CTA after the grid.
- Depth: soft green-tinted shadows on cards; hover is a color shift, never a scale jump.
- Never: emoji or glyph icons (lucide only), text-heavy sections, low-contrast gray text, raw colors outside the theme.
