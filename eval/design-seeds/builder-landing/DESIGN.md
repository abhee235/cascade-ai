# Design direction — Ferrite

Mood: a calm, precise operations tool for on-call engineers. Dark slate, high readability, no hype.

- Color: red (`primary`) is THE call to action and nothing else; green means healthy/resolved only; everything else is neutral slate. Never decorate with red or green.
- Type: headlines in JetBrains Mono (the display face, `font-serif`) with tight tracking; body in IBM Plex Sans; numbers and timestamps in mono.
- Hero: the product itself — a realistic incident preview (grouped events, severities, times) beside a one-line promise, one red CTA and one quiet action.
- Sections: key metrics (3–4 numbers with labels) → how it works (3 steps) → social proof → closing CTA band. The primary CTA also sits in the nav.
- Rhythm: spacious (the theme's 96/128 px sections); cards on `bg-card` with a 1 px `border`; minimal glow, no gradients.
- Honesty: label any "live" data with its update time; show error and empty states.
- Never: decorative charts, emoji or glyph icons, real companies as fake customers, low-contrast gray-on-slate text, raw colors outside the theme.
